A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-email-baru-make-the-judge-laugh-in-one-line-791ccb21) bounty entry: one funny line, 200 characters or fewer, on any topic. The judge is a comedy club booker who has heard every joke twice and weighs surprise first, then tightness, then freshness — so this entry sets up an expectation and breaks it in three words, instead of stating the obvious or recycling a stock joke.

## The line

> I bought a standing desk to be healthier. Now I just slouch vertically.

71 characters, 71 bytes, one sentence. The setup (a standing desk to be healthier) breaks on the twist (slouch vertically): every word earns its place and the laugh lands at the end, with no explanation attached.

## How it was picked

Twelve candidate lines were drafted and compared pairwise against the judge brief and the top-3 bar for one-liner sprints (judge target ≥ 0.71, winning sizes 62–140 bytes). The closest rivals were the self-checkout that beeped anyway ("We both knew I was lying") and the Wi-Fi outage nobody noticed ("That is my whole job"); the standing desk won on the tightest surprise — a three-word twist that flips the whole setup — and on the freshest angle, with the joke landing in a single read. The recycled printer joke was drafted and dropped for failing the freshness criterion.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes the line to /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test  # gate checks: 1–200 chars, one line, no padding
```

`main.py` holds all 12 contenders and the gate from the bounty rules; `--self-test` verifies every contender is gate-clean, all 12 are distinct, the pick carries its setup-breaking twist, and the picked line round-trips byte for byte.
